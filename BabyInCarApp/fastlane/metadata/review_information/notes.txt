Hi Apple Reviewer — thanks for reviewing Soothbee 1.3.0.

Soothbee is a baby cry detection and soothing app for parents. To test the headline cry-detection feature:

1. Open the app and complete the brief baby profile setup (any name + DOB works).
2. Tap the 'Detect' tab in the bottom bar.
3. Tap 'Start' on the cry detection card.
4. Play any baby-crying sound near the device microphone (a short YouTube clip works fine). Soothbee should detect within ~2 seconds and begin playing a soothing track automatically.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.3.0
- Polished honeycomb halos around cry-detection feedback so confidence reads at a glance
- Logo-first empty states with the bee mascot in Favorites, Playlists, and the queue
- Bigger tap target on the mini player for one-handed pause/resume
- Russian Siri vocabulary — Russian-locale users can say "Привет, Siri, включи колыбельные в Soothbee"

ACCESS
No login is required to test all features. The Premium subscription unlocks the full content library; basic playback and cry detection work without Premium.

PRIVACY
All cry-detection AI runs entirely on-device using Apple's Sound Analysis framework. Audio content (lullabies, fairy tales) streams from our CDN. No personal data or audio recordings leave the device for AI inference.

CARPLAY
The app supports CarPlay. To test, connect to a CarPlay-enabled simulator or vehicle.
