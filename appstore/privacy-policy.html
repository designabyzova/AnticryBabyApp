Baby in Car - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

Introduction

Baby in Car ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy and the privacy of your family. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our baby soothing application.

Privacy First: Baby in Car is designed with privacy at its core. Most features work entirely on your device, and we minimize data collection to what is absolutely necessary.

Information We Collect

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Baby Profile: Name, age, preferences - stored only on your device
  • Sound Preferences: Favorites, effectiveness ratings - stored locally
  • Cry Patterns: Audio analysis for detection - processed on-device, never uploaded
  • Usage History: Played tracks, session data - stored locally

Information We May Collect (Optional)

  • Anonymous Analytics: Crash reports and basic usage statistics to improve the app (opt-in)
  • Subscription Data: Managed by Apple, not stored by us

Microphone Usage

Baby in Car uses your device's microphone for:

  • Cry Detection: To detect when your baby is crying and automatically play soothing sounds
  • Voice Commands: For hands-free control while driving

Important: Audio is processed entirely on your device. We do NOT record, store, or transmit audio data to any server. No audio ever leaves your device.

How We Use Information

  • To personalize your baby's soothing experience
  • To remember your preferences and favorites
  • To improve cry detection accuracy for your specific baby
  • To provide hands-free voice control
  • To improve app stability (anonymous crash reports, if opted in)

Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, share, or transfer your personal data to third parties.

The only exceptions are:

  • Apple: For subscription management and App Store services
  • Crash Reporting: Anonymous technical data if you opt-in

Children's Privacy

Baby in Car is designed for parents to use with their children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. All baby-related information is stored locally on the parent's device and controlled by the parent.

Data Security

  • All data is stored locally on your device using iOS secure storage
  • Network communications use HTTPS encryption
  • We follow Apple's security best practices

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: All data is on your device - view in app settings
  • Delete: Uninstall the app to remove all local data
  • Opt-out: Disable analytics in app settings
  • Control: Manage all permissions in iOS Settings

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app and updating the "Last Updated" date.
