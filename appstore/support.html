Frequently Asked Questions

How does cry detection work?

Baby in Car uses advanced AI to analyze audio from your device's microphone and detect baby crying. When crying is detected, soothing sounds automatically play. All processing happens on your device - no audio is ever uploaded or stored.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes! Once you've downloaded sounds to your device, they work completely offline. Cry detection also works offline. Perfect for car rides through areas with poor coverage.

How do I use the app while driving?

Baby in Car supports CarPlay for safe, hands-free operation. You can also use voice commands by saying "Hey, play lullaby" or "Stop" while the app is running. Never operate your phone manually while driving.

Why isn't cry detection working?

Please check:

  • Microphone permission is granted in Settings > Baby in Car > Microphone
  • Cry detection is enabled in the app (Cry Detection tab)
  • Device volume isn't muted
  • The device is positioned where it can hear the baby

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel:

  1. Open iPhone Settings
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Find Baby in Car and tap Cancel

What's included in the free version?

The free version includes:

  • Basic sound library (30+ sounds)
  • Cry detection (3 times per day)
  • Voice commands
  • Favorites

Premium unlocks unlimited cry detection, full library (100+ sounds), AI recommendations, CarPlay, and more.

Is my data private?

Yes! All your data (baby profiles, preferences, usage history) is stored locally on your device. Audio from cry detection is never recorded, stored, or transmitted. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Troubleshooting

App Won't Start

  • Make sure your iPhone is running iOS 16 or later
  • Try force-closing and reopening the app
  • Restart your device
  • Reinstall the app from the App Store

Sounds Not Playing

  • Check that your device isn't in silent mode
  • Increase the volume
  • If using Bluetooth, check the connection
  • Try a different sound

CarPlay Not Working

  • Ensure your car supports CarPlay
  • Check the Lightning/USB connection
  • Grant CarPlay permissions in iOS Settings
  • Try reconnecting your iPhone
