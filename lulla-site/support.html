Frequently Asked Questions

How does cry detection work?

Lulla uses on-device machine learning (Apple's Sound Analysis framework + DeepInfant AI model) to detect baby cries. When a cry is detected, the app automatically plays soothing sounds. All processing happens on your device — no audio is sent to any server.

Does the app work offline?

Yes! Once you've downloaded your favorite tracks, the app works completely offline. Cry detection and voice commands also work without an internet connection.

How do I use CarPlay?

Simply connect your iPhone to your CarPlay-enabled vehicle. Lulla will appear on your CarPlay dashboard. You can browse categories, control playback, and use voice commands hands-free.

What voice commands are supported?

You can say things like:

  • "Play lullabies"
  • "Play classical music"
  • "Next track" / "Previous track"
  • "Make it louder" / "Make it quieter"
  • "Stop" / "Pause" / "Resume"

Why are there no rain or thunder sounds?

Based on research and parent feedback, harsh weather sounds can be startling or scary for babies. Our sound library focuses on gentle, soothing content like lullabies, classical music, and peaceful ambient sounds.

How do I mark a track as a favorite?

While playing a track, tap the heart icon. Your favorites are saved and easily accessible from the Favorites tab.

Can I download tracks for offline use?

Yes! Tap the download icon on any track or playlist to save it for offline listening. Premium subscribers get unlimited offline downloads.

Troubleshooting

Cry detection isn't working

Make sure:

  • Microphone permission is enabled in Settings > Lulla
  • Your device's microphone isn't blocked
  • Background App Refresh is enabled for the app

Audio isn't playing

Check that:

  • Your device isn't in Silent mode
  • Volume is turned up
  • No other app is actively using audio (Lulla will pause other apps)
