2. Description of Service

Lulla is a baby soothing application that provides:

  • AI-powered cry detection (processed entirely on your device)
  • A library of soothing sounds, lullabies, classical music, and fairy tales
  • CarPlay integration for hands-free use while driving
  • Personalized recommendations based on effectiveness tracking

3. AI and Data Processing

The App uses Apple's CoreML framework and Sound Analysis framework for cry detection. All AI processing occurs entirely on your device:

  • No audio data is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted to any external server
  • No third-party AI service is used
  • The App does not send personal data to AI services for processing
  • Cry detection models are bundled within the App and run locally
