Questions, answered

Things other parents ask first.

How does Soothbee know why my baby is crying?
We use Apple's on-device Sound Analysis framework paired with our DeepInfant model. It recognises seven cry types — hungry, tired, discomfort, pain, lonely, scared, burping — in under a second. Everything happens on your iPhone. No audio is ever uploaded.
Is anything about my baby sent to a server?
No. The microphone, the AI, the decision and the sound all live on your phone. We have no account system, no analytics and no way to see inside your home. If your phone is offline, Soothbee still works.
Will it eat my battery?
Listening mode uses about as much battery as a podcast app running in the background — roughly 4–6% per hour on a modern iPhone. You can pause it in one tap whenever you like.
Does CarPlay really work hands-free?
Yes — Soothbee detects the cry and starts a calming track on your car speakers without you touching anything. You can say "Hey Soothbee, quieter" to adjust. It was designed for the school run.
Can I use Siri to control it?
Yes. "Hey Siri, play something calmer on Soothbee" and similar commands are supported. We also ship Shortcuts for common flows like "night feed".
Is it really free? What about Premium?
Core cry detection, CarPlay and a starter library are free, always. Premium ($6.99/mo, $49.99/yr, or $149.99 lifetime) unlocks the full 270-track library, offline downloads, all languages and advanced AI. Cancel anytime — Apple handles refunds.
