## Tips for Sending

1. **Find the right contact:**
   - Check their official website m-w-m.ru for contact form
   - Look for business/partnership email
   - Try reaching through their social media (VK, etc.)

2. **Timing:** Send during business hours (Moscow time)

3. **Follow-up:** If no response in 7-10 days, send a polite follow-up

4. **Attachments to prepare:**
   - App screenshots or demo video
   - Company registration documents (if applicable)
   - Brief presentation (3-5 slides)

5. **Be prepared to:**
   - Sign an NDA if requested
   - Provide proof of business legitimacy
   - Negotiate terms patiently
