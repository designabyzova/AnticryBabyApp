2026 Algorithm Reality (Fresh This Quarter)

TikTok

  • Completion rate is now ~40–50% of ranking signal, with a 70% bar for viral lift (up from 50% in 2024). Proof: Sprout Social 2026 algorithm teardown.
  • Longer formats (1–3 min) get ~2x more distribution than sub-30s when retention holds. But a 15s fully-watched still beats a 60s half-watched.
  • TikTok SEO is decisive: the algorithm indexes spoken words, on-screen text, captions, and hashtags. Say the keyword aloud.
  • Shares and saves outweigh likes. DM-shares carry the highest weight.
  • Soothbee play: 45–75s explainers with spoken keyword density ("why is my baby crying", "infant hunger cues") + on-screen text + caption SEO (triple-indexing).

Instagram

  • Mosseri's confirmed 2026 trio: watch time, likes-per-reach, sends-per-reach. DM sends are the strongest new-audience reach signal.
  • Reels win reach for accounts under 50K (median 134 views vs. 56 for carousels under 1K). Carousels win engagement (1.92% vs. 0.50% Reels) and outperform Reels on accounts >1M.
  • Hashtags are now categorization-only — 3–5 max. Keyword SEO in captions matters more.
  • Trial Reels (post-to-non-followers-first) is live — use for testing angles.
  • Broadcast Channels are a strong fit for parenting; high open rates, low lift. Create one called "Soothbee Sleep Notes."

YouTube

  • Shorts length cap stays at 3 minutes.
  • Monetization: 1K subs + 10M Shorts views/90 days or 4K long-form hours for ad revenue. New entry tier at 500 subs + 3M Shorts views unlocks fan funding only.
  • Shorts pay $0.03–0.10/1K views. Long-form pays 5–10x more. The Shorts-to-long-form funnel is the real play.
  • Soothbee play: Shorts of cry-detection demos + 8–12 min founder/pediatrician deep-dives.

Pinterest

  • Inaugural 2026 Parenting Trend Report is Pinterest's first — a free gift. Key trends: "screen-free activities" +200%, "slow motherhood" +310%, "positive discipline" +295%.
  • Idea Pins are deprecated. Pinterest merged standard Pins + Idea Pins into one unified format. Existing multi-page Idea Pins are preserved as videos.
  • Fresh pins beat repins. Vertical 1000x1500 static + short vertical video.
  • Soothbee play: Cry-decoder infographic series + "slow motherhood" aesthetic carousels.

X / Twitter

  • Non-Premium link posts get near-zero median reach as of March 2026.
  • Premium accounts see ~0.25–0.3% engagement on links — viable but thin.
  • Feed is Grok-sorted by default; chronological toggle exists but few use it.
  • Community Notes cause 60–80% reach cuts when attached.
  • Verdict: Pay Premium + post founder-voice threads, or skip entirely.

Facebook

  • Page organic reach ~0.07% (effectively dead).
  • Group reach 5–15%.
  • Verdict: Worth it only through Groups. Have Anton (or a moderator) participate authentically in existing "new mom," "colic survivors," "tired parents" groups. Don't build a Soothbee Group from zero.

Threads vs. Bluesky

  • Threads: 400M MAU, Meta ad-tech backbone, For You cold-push, auto-imports IG follows. Invest.
  • Bluesky: 35M MAU, slower-moving, higher-trust, custom feeds. Skip for now — revisit only if a parenting-safety sub-community forms there.

LinkedIn (Founder-Led Only)

  • 2026 algorithm weights "Dwell Time" (45+ sec reads).
  • Founder posts about building (AI ethics, infant safety, the journey) outperform product posts.
  • Good for Tier 2 tech press (The Information, TechCrunch reporters lurk) and angel-stage investor visibility.
  • Low ROI for user acquisition. Use only if you want press/investors.
