08 · Technology & Moat

The only consumer app with <1s cry classification on-device.

  • DeepInfant V2 — custom 3-stage pipeline classifier (VGGish preprocessing → Int8-quantized CNN → GLM head). 5.1 MB. Apple Create ML trained on a proprietary cry corpus.
  • Apple Sound Analysis — we ride Apple's newest on-device audio framework. Zero infra, zero GPU bill, automatic OS-level optimization.
  • 270-track curated corpus — proprietary safety-scored audio library. A forbidden-content list keeps harsh, scary, and stimulating sounds out (rain, vacuum, white noise — all banned by pediatric audio research).
